About

Find a place

A global archive of graffiti art — documenting what has been painted at spots around the world, so nothing is forgotten.

When someone paints over an existing piece at a spot, they can upload a photo of the new work. Over time, each spot builds up a visual history of everything that has ever been there.

Anyone can upload photos and add spots. The map is open to the whole world.

💡 When you upload a photo, you receive a personal edit code. Save it — you can use it later to edit or delete your own upload. If you lose it, contact the map admin.
